## How this repository was made
- Built with **Claude** (Anthropic's AI assistant) as a personal study aid for system design interviews.
- Only the publicly visible course roadmap and lesson titles were used as reference.
- The premium lesson bodies were **not** accessed. No text, code, diagrams, numbers or headings from
  them are reproduced. Explanations, estimates, diagrams, follow-up answers and exercises were written
  independently from publicly known engineering concepts and public documentation/papers.
- Section flow inside each problem (Clarify → Estimate → APIs → HLD → Database → Deep Dive → Follow-ups)
  follows the widely used interview structure, mirrored from the roadmap's lesson outline.
